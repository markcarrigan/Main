# Exploring markcarrigan.net: An AI Engagement

This repository contains the output of an experiment: asking Claude (Anthropic) to explore [markcarrigan.net](https://markcarrigan.net)—a blog maintained since 2010 with over 5,500 posts on digital sociology, critical realism, platform capitalism, and the accelerated academy—and to create something that demonstrates AI capabilities while potentially enriching intellectual work.

## Contents

### 1. `INTELLECTUAL_DIALOGUE.md`
A substantive engagement with themes from the blog, including:
- Critical examination of the "variety machine" framing for AI
- Extension of digital distraction theory to AI-mediated attention
- Application of Archer's reflexivity framework to human-AI interaction
- Self-critical analysis of what AI can and cannot offer intellectual work

### 2. `blog_explorer/`
A Python tool for exploring long-form blog archives:
- Full-text search with relevance ranking
- Similarity detection across posts
- Temporal concept tracing
- Random rediscovery for surfacing forgotten posts
- Writing prompt generation

Inspired by C. Wright Mills' "On Intellectual Craftsmanship."

### 3. `blog_explorer/visualizations/`
Interactive concept mapping tools using D3.js to visualise thematic relationships across the archive.

### 4. `WHAT_I_MADE.md`
Summary and rationale for what was created and why.

## Context

Mark Carrigan is a Senior Lecturer in Education at the University of Manchester, author of *Social Media for Academics* and *Generative AI for Academics*, and co-founder of the BSA's Digital Sociology group. His blog represents 14+ years of public intellectual work.

This repository emerged from a single prompt: "Please have a thorough exploration then consider what you could do with it in order to demonstrate your capabilities and enrich my intellectual life."
